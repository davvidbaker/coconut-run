WELCOME TO THE WORKSHOP

A good idea on wheels.

1. Build. Drag beams and blocks to make a basket. Touching frame pieces join. Draw wheels by dragging outward from their centers; wheels overlapping a frame get an axle automatically.

2. Connect. Drag suspension between two parts for a flexible connection. Use larger wheels to climb obstacles. Undo or erase anything.

3. Load & drive. Test your design, release 15 coconuts, then hold → or D to drive and ← or A to reverse. On touchscreens, hold the on-screen arrows.

4. Go nuts. Hold Shift or the boost button for a burst of speed. Your tank recharges when you ease off. Collect three stars, chase gold medals, and beat your best on each course. Press T for a quick retry.

Deliver coconuts into the shredder at the finish. Your score rewards cargo and speed. Return to the workshop anytime to improve your design.
